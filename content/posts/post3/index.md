## Download my app!

Well its been a year since [KartStopper](https://kartstopper.netlify.app/) was announced in my [first post](../hello-world) and here we are in a post **iPhone Duo**[^copyright] era. I am glad to announce that it has been published on the **App Store**[^copyright]. You may [download](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kartstopper/id6813073115?itscg=30200&itsct=apps_box_link&mttnsubad=6813073115) the iOS[^copyright] app right away.

## Do you own an iPhone Duo?
A foldable device presenting tabular data and graphs next to each other effortlessly just like a physical book of balance sheets would be so cool. Isn't it? Only if the device lasts long enough to track the data without cracking at the hinge, alas. It used to be fun to flip a feature phone back in the day but folding an iPhone is [reportedly fragile](https://www.pcmag.com/news/iphone-duo-owners-are-reporting-cracks-in-their-screens-users-call-it-crackgate)!
